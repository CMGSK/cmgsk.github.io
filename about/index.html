Hello,

my name is Carlos Manso. I'm a Technical Lead and Senior Software Engineer, currently working at F.Hoffmann-LaRoche AG. I'm based on Madrid (to my dismay) but I was born in Asturias, where I lived for nearly 27 years.

Right now, I'm mainly working in Rust, Python, Typescript, and PostgreSQL. Additionally, we use Docker, Kubernetes, Git, and other common technologies

I started learning programming in 2022, after finishing my Media Production High Diploma, when I was unemployed and looking for a full-time job on the field. Since I was very bored and doing nothing with my life, a good friend of mine offered himself to teach me how to code, and I accepted.

I was also learning the ropes on Linux as well at that time, so he told me to open a "test.cpp" file with Vim, and from there on, I got obsessed with learning how to code. We did some Advent of Code, he taught be about loops, compilers, pointers, memory management and almost everything I was going to need to be a good programmer, but without an IDE. To the current day, I'm still grateful to him. I have a job, a future, and a pretty good life thanks to him and how he decided to teach me.

After learning the basics for C++, I decided that since I was unemployed, I'd better study than do nothing. I joined a Vocational Training for a High Diploma in Software development, where I learnt Java, Typescript, SQL, and other common technologies, and three months into classes, I got my first job as a developer.

Although I hated the stack they were using, it was nice experiencing what was a real 8h stable job, with a decent, "over-the-counter" salary, and I am very grateful that they gave me that oportunity, even though I was a completely unexperienced guy without any qualification.

From that job on, I've done a lot of things, but essentially, I've come to love learning new things. I started studying Japanese (which is a language, a country and a culture I really love), I learnt some C#, Lua, Python, did my Higher Diploma Final Project on training myself a Convolutional Neural Network in Python, among other things.

I still find some spare days to create some personal projects I find interesting, although if I'm completely honest, I rarely finish them.

Other things about me are that like I love Hatsune Miku and have a big collection of her merch, I like JPop and JCore music, reading, cooking and photograpy... And that's about it.

Thank you for your interest and for paying me a visit. See you!
